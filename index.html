On the Nature of Corban Pendrak (contested)

Corban Pendrak is a humanoid-looking being, but it is debated whether his true nature leans more eldritch or siren (Pendrak et al.). Observation of his habits and general demeanor leans eldritch, though many close encounters have reported a strange, subtle manipulation reminiscent of sirens (Various personal encounters). While his age on this planet is unknown, he is truly unfathomably old (see other notes). His reflexes are often compared with an octopus and his eyes like blobs, “his eyes shimmered like blobs”, though it is not known what this actually means (Enoon et al.). Photographs often reveal a highly photogenic being aware of most cameras in his vicinity (see various footage).
